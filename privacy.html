Iron Treads - Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 - applies to the iOS and Android versions of Iron Treads ("the game") published by Arcade Works.

Iron Treads is a single-player arcade tank-defense game. It has no account, no login, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own. The only data processing in the game comes from the advertising network that serves the short interstitial advertisements shown at some round-result and defeat screens. This page explains what that involves and the choices you have.

1. Data the game itself stores

Your scores, round checkpoint, unlocked modes, records, options (sound, haptics, controls, graphics quality) and the two counters that decide when the next advertisement break may occur are saved on your device only, in the game's own storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Interstitial advertisements are served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). An advertisement may be requested after every third round you clear and after every third run you lose; it is shown only on the result or defeat screen, never during combat. The SDK may pre-load the next advertisement in the background so it is ready at that moment. No other advertising or mediation network is used.

To serve, deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

3. Your choices

In the game

If you play from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before the first advertisement is requested. You can change your choices at any time under SETTINGS > AD PRIVACY, which appears whenever privacy options are available for your region. Declining personalised ads does not limit the game in any way.

On iOS

The game does not ask for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency and never reads the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). Advertising in the game is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the App Store privacy label reflects this. Google may use Apple's SKAdNetwork, a privacy-preserving attribution system that does not identify you.

On Android

You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads (on some devices Settings > Privacy > Ads), and turn off ad personalisation for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

Playing offline

The game is fully playable without a network connection. Without a connection no advertisement is requested or shown.

4. Children

Iron Treads is not directed at children under 13 and carries an age rating for cartoon violence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the advertising network is configured for a general audience.

5. Data retention and transfers

The game stores nothing about you on our servers - we operate none. Google retains the data described above according to its own policies and may process it in the United States and other countries under appropriate safeguards (for EU/UK users, standard contractual clauses). Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, California and other US states, Brazil, and others) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to profiling, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Because we hold no data ourselves, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google at the links above; you can also exercise the choices described in section 3 directly. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. For anything else, contact us below.

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
