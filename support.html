How to play

Slide the left pad to drive and lift your thumb to stop. Hold FIRE to shoot while moving. Tap EMP to stop every enemy engine and clear hostile shells. Tap the pause icon to pause. You can move, fire and use EMP with separate fingers at the same time. The four-page field manual is available from the main menu and the pause menu.

Controls, sound and graphics

Settings lets you mirror the movement and action controls for left-handed play, choose Balanced or Rich graphics, and toggle sound, haptics, camera shake and the white player marker. TEST SOUND confirms audio is working. Game audio ignores the ring/silent switch; the media volume and your headphones still apply.

Progress, Continue and New Game

Progress is saved on your device at the start of each round. Continue restarts the saved round from its beginning with your score, tank upgrades, reserves and command-post health from earlier rounds intact - it does not resume mid-battle. New Game starts from round 1 and asks for confirmation before replacing a saved run. Deleting the app deletes all progress and records; there is no cloud backup.

Boss Rush

Boss Rush unlocks permanently once you finish round 30. It also unlocks on an existing save that had already completed the game. Retry restarts the whole rush from the first boss.

Advertisements

A short interstitial ad may appear after every third round you clear and after every third run you lose - only on the result or defeat screen, never during combat. If no ad is available the screen simply continues. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you can change your consent choices under Settings > Ad Privacy. Playing without a network connection shows no ads at all. See the Privacy Policy for what the ad network collects.

Something looks wrong?

If the game freezes, a button stops responding, or an ad does not close, force-quit the app and reopen it - your saved round is kept. Then email us with the details above so we can fix it.

Privacy

Iron Treads has no account, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Read the full Privacy Policy.
